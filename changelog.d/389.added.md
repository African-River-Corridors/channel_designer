- **`owd envelope`: the largest vessel the river allows (#389, spec 0016 T2).** A sweep of single-hull motor vessels, 10 × 2, 20 × 4, 30 × 6, 40 × 8 and 50 × 10 m by default (`--sizes 10x2,…`), all at a 2 m draught (`--draught`), under PIANC WG 141 quality C (`--rule-set`). Each size gets its own fairway fit, seeded from the reference line, in the water less half its bottom width, with its own R_min, and the width and the radius are read on it, as `owd fits` reads them. The answer: the largest size that passes the whole reach, the largest that passes each kilometre, each size's blocked stretches, and the 12 default classes and the custom vessel marked against the largest size by length and beam. As a table, `--json`, `--csv` (the per-kilometre strip) and `--png FILE` (a chart of each size along the river, and the strip). Every output says "Screening — not for construction", the rule set, the draught and that the banks can set the limit. The study keeps it as `stages.envelope`; it is stale when the banks, the river polygon, the reference line, the custom vessel or the settings change, and only `owd envelope` replaces it. With a bed model, its shallowest assumed depth is reported beside the answer. On the whole Sassandra (139 km reference line) the sweep takes about 20 s.
