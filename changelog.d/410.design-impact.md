- **Banks that crossed after the checks move (#410).** What moved: where a reach's generated banks still crossed after the repair rounds, the banks, the centreline, the widths and the low-confidence stretches move there. On the recorded whole-river Sassandra banks (`midline-tangle.json`), 12 crossings go to 0. The pinch repair takes 12 to 4. The untangle then replaces 2 pieces, 737 m of bank, moved up to 111 m (a gap chord). The centreline stays within 10 % of its seed. Why: a crossing of the two banks is a wrong bank, not the river; it failed the reach (`banks_cross`). Banks that do not cross do not move. The design-impact scenarios do not use generated banks, so they show no change.
