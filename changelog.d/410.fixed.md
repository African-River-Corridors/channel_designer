- **Generated banks no longer cross after the checks (#410).** The bank checks' rounds could leave the two banks crossed: a zero-width pinch, a spike in one bank, or a straight gap chord across the other bank. `owd reach` then failed with `banks_cross`. Now two steps fix that. The round after a crossing repairs a zero-width pinch across both banks (flagged `side`). After the last round, a direct untangle with no rebuild takes each pair of crossings: the wrong piece between them (the one with a gap chord, else the more winding one) is replaced by the other bank's piece, laid 2 m inside it. Only a crossing with no partner is left, still flagged `crossing`. What the untangle moved is in the reach JSON, as `banks.untangle` and `edge_fit.untangle` (pieces, `max_move_m`, `moved_m`, `crossings_left`), and in the text output, so a big move is visible. Banks that do not cross come out unchanged.
