- **The reference line (#388, spec 0016 T1).** One smooth line in the water that belongs to no vessel. OWD makes it from the two bank lines: a mid-line of the banks (each point paired forward only, at most five 20 m steps ahead), smoothed by two Chaikin passes and Taubin smoothing, then refitted in the water by the fairway fit. The refit holds the line off the banks by the larger of 5 m and 10 % of the local width, has no minimum radius, and pulls the line towards the mid-line with one weight, tuned on two windows and fixed in code with its label. The study stores it as `inputs.reference_line`, with its settings, the banks' low-confidence stretches carried onto it, the stretches where the water is too narrow to hold it, and a hash of what it was made from: a change to the banks, the river polygon or the CRS makes it stale, and the next `owd fits` makes it again. No stage reads it, so storing it leaves the stages fresh.
