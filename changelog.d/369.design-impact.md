- **Radius stretches of design line options (#369).** What moved: the radius stretches of every screened design line option (`stages.volumes.results.options[].screening`), and with them `stretch_count` and the verdict where radius stretches were the only works. Which way: fewer. An option's line already meets R_min where it can (its `radius_ok` check), so the radius stretches that read the river's own bends go. On the web example the rolling-circle option's 3 radius stretches went to 0; its width, depth and infeasible stretches do not move. A line tighter than R_min still shows its stretch. `owd fits` does not move: it reads the radius on its plan line as before. Why: the radius was read on the river centreline where each station projected onto it, so a line that cuts across a bend took the bend's radius.
