- **App: find a river as you type.** In "Start from a river" (desktop and web), typing 3 letters lists the matching rivers and canals as "river — region, country", with an optional country filter. Choosing one draws the whole river, sets Start at its mouth and End at its upstream end, and shows its length. Drag Start or End on the map, or pick them, to cut a reach out of the same river with no new request. (#308)
