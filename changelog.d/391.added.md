- **The app's "Largest vessel" panel (spec 0016 T4, #391).** In "Start from a river", step 7, **Find the largest vessel** runs the size sweep of `owd envelope` (spec 0016 T2) through the shell, so the desktop and the web app share one path. The defaults are five single hulls, 10 × 2 m to 50 × 10 m, PIANC-C and a 2 m draught. The panel shows:
  - the answer for the whole reach;
  - the series table: R_min, bottom width, and whether each size passes, in words and a shape;
  - a chart of where each size is blocked along the river, with the largest size per kilometre;
  - the per-kilometre list;
  - the catalogue classes against the result;
  - the screening notice and the bank caveat.
  The map draws each kilometre of the plan line coloured by the largest size that passes it, from the smallest of the series to the largest, and in the fail colour where none passes. The shell gives the strip in WGS84 as `reach_view.envelope_strip`, and the snapshot gains `envelope_status`. Like `fits`, the sweep never reruns by itself: a change to the river makes it stale.
