- **A design line option's radius stretches read its own line (#369).** The screening of an option read the radius on the river centreline where each station projected onto it, so a straight option across the meanders still showed the river's bends as radius stretches. The minimum radius is now checked on the option's own line, and on the plan line where `fits` gives one (the reference line, a fairway fit or the centreline). Every screening says which in `radius_read_on`: `the design line option` or `the plan line`.
