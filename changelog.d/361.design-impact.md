- **Generated banks on narrow rivers, and their low-confidence stretches (#361).** What moved: `low_confidence_width.stretches` of generated banks (fewer: overlapping and touching stretches are one; a merged stretch takes the most serious kind and the narrowest width), and so the reports' "Data and limits" counts and the low-confidence marks on the reference line. On a river narrower than 60 m (median mask width), `--edge-fit` now keeps the mask banks, so its banks, centreline, widths and everything read on them are the mask's. Which way: on the recorded Sassandra estuary window, 3 stretches → 1 (all three lay at one place, the end of the checked centreline). Why: the stretches of two sources were joined without merging, and the edge fit cannot resolve a river a few pixels wide. The design-impact scenarios do not use generated banks, so they show no change.
