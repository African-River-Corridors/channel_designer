- **`owd fits --draught M` (#364).** Sizes every catalogue class at one draught, such as a laden draught, instead of each class's own, as `owd size --draught` does for one class. It sets each class's design depth (and anything else the Rule Sets read from the draught), in the plan-only answer and the full one. A custom vessel keeps its own draught. The answer and its settings keep `draught_m`, so a change makes the answer stale, and the table says `Draught: M m for every class`. Without it, nothing changes.
