- **Each study saves itself.** On the desktop, a new study from a river is saved at once in its own folder in the projects folder (`~/Documents/Open Waterway Designer/`, or `OWD_PROJECTS_DIR`), named `<date> <River> km <start>-<end>`, for example `2026-10-22 Rhine km 0-1006`. The kilometres count from the river's mouth. Re-cutting a study that holds only its reach renames its folder. The document and every download go inside it, each reach step saves it, and Fetch DEM never asks to save first. Save as still works; the web app keeps its browser storage. (#322)
