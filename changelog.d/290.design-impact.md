- **The bank checks on a whole river (#290, spec 0013 T6).** The first run of the Sassandra worked example on the Sentinel-2 seed showed three faults that a short window did not, now fixed. Each round of the checks read the banks along the last round's centreline, so a round that went wrong made the next one's reference worse: the edge-fitted centreline came out at 1,731 km for a 144 km river, with 26,000 `crossing` flags. Every round now reads along the original seed. A repair was spliced into the bank at the nearest vertex found over the whole bank, which on a long bank could match a vertex far along, broke the order and rebuilt the whole bank from its offsets; the search now walks forward from the last splice, and a run it cannot place is left as it is (and stays flagged). That search was also what made the run take 88 minutes. And a bank that runs on past either end of the centreline (a cast that found other water at the first or last station) is trimmed there, since no check can see it. What moved: generated banks, centrelines, widths and stretches wherever these happened, and the regenerated worked example. The design-impact scenarios do not use generated banks, so they show no change.
