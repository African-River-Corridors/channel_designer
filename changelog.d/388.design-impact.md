- **`owd fits` reads the river on the reference line (#388, ADR 0014).** What moved: every `fits` answer made again with the default plan line. The width, radius and depth stretches, `length_m`, `stretch_count`, the worst deficits, `fits`, the verdict and `largest_fitting` can all change, because they are read along the reference line, not along the centreline (plan only) or each class's fairway (full). Which way, on the whole Sassandra (179 km centreline, 144 km route; the reference line is 139 km): plan only, radius stretches over the 12 classes 13,779 → 966 and width stretches 1,942 → 676; full, radius stretches 3,286 on the fairway (12,065 on the centreline) → 1,382, and width stretches 2,067 → 613. Depth moves the other way for the shallow classes: the bed model is a parabola that is deepest on the study's centreline, and the reference line does not follow that line's wiggle, so it reads shallower where the two part. The depth stretches of the classes that need 2.1 m and 2.6 m went from 1 to 39 and 96; the classes that need more than the 3 m assumed stay one stretch. On the synthetic example (plan only, 40 m wide), width stretches 50 → 47, and no class fits on either line. The full answer also ran faster on the Sassandra: 41 s, against 156 s on the centreline and 262 s on the fairway. A study without both bank lines reads the centreline, as before, with a note. Answers already stored keep their line until `owd fits` runs again. The design-impact scenarios do not run `fits`, so they show no change.
