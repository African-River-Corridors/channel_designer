- **`fits` can read the depth from a DEM that holds the river bed (#363).** `owd import --dem FILE --holds-bathymetry` marks the DEM as holding surveyed bathymetry below the water level (`inputs.dem.holds_bathymetry: true`). With the mark and a water level, `owd fits` gives the full answer with no bed model, and reads the depth from the DEM as it is. Every output that reads it carries "depth from the DEM's bathymetry, as marked by the user, not checked". OWD does not check the DEM. A new DEM clears the mark. A bed model, if there is one, still wins and keeps its "depth assumed, not measured" label. Without the mark nothing changes.
