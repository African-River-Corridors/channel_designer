- **The assumed bed is centred on the reference line (#398).** The bed model of `owd bed` (spec 0012 T7) is a parabola across the water, deepest on its axis. Its axis was the study's centreline, which is drawn from the banks and wiggles, so a line off it read the bed as shallower than the middle of the river. When the study holds a fresh reference line (spec 0016), the parabola is now centred on it. The depth table and the water level still read the river chainage (the centreline's). What moved: every depth read from an assumed bed in a study with a reference line: `owd fits` depth stretches, verdicts and `largest_fitting`, and the volumes stage (dredge, dry cut and their stretches), which now goes stale when a reference line is made or changes. Which way: deeper along the middle of the river, so fewer depth stretches. On the whole Sassandra (3 m assumed, full `fits` on the reference line) the depth stretches over the 12 classes went from 240 to 9: the classes that need 2.1 m and 2.6 m have none (they had 39 and 96), and each class that needs more than 3 m keeps one. Radius and width do not move. A study with no reference line does not move. The design-impact scenarios have no reference line, so they show no change.
