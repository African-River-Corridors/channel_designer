- **`owd fits` reads the river on the reference line by default (#388, ADR 0014).** Width, radius, depth and the reaches are all read along it, in the plan-only answer and the full one, in the command line, the desktop app and the web app. `--plan-line fairway` and `--plan-line centreline` still run. The answer says `plan_line: "reference"` and `Plan line: reference` in the table. A study without both bank lines reads the centreline, and `plan_line_note` says why. ADR 0013's default (the fairway) is superseded.
