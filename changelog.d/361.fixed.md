- **The edge fit on narrow rivers, and the low-confidence count (#361).** On a river about 40 m wide (four 10 m Sentinel-2 pixels) the edge fit made the banks worse. It is now not run on a river whose median mask width is under 60 m (provisional): `owd reach --edge-fit` keeps the mask banks, says why on stderr and in the JSON (`edge_fit.skipped`), and the desktop and web apps show the same reason. And the low-confidence stretches from the mask or the edge fit and from the bank checks are merged where they overlap or touch, into one stretch of the most serious kind and the narrowest width, so there are never more stretches than stations (a run had 4,208 for 1,634 stations).
